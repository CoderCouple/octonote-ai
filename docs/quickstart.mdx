---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Your first note, canvas and published page in five minutes."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [octonote.ai](https://octonote.ai/signup) and continue with Google, Apple, or your email (we send a 6-digit code). Octonote creates your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a note">
    Open **Notes** and create one. Type `/` for blocks: headings, lists, code, an **Equation**, a **Mermaid diagram** and more. Everything saves as you type. See [Notes](/guides/notes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw on a canvas">
    Open **Canvases** and create one. Turn on **Auto-shape** and rough pencil strokes snap into clean boxes, circles and arrows. See [Canvases](/guides/canvases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put them side by side">
    A **project** is one note and one canvas together. Group shapes into a **figure** and drag it into the note. See [Projects](/guides/projects).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Click **Share**, turn on **Publish to web**, and copy the public link. Anyone can read it, no account needed. See [Publishing](/guides/publishing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Let your agent do the first draft

If you use Claude Code or Codex, connect them once and ask them to write for you:

```bash
npm i -g @octonote/cli
octonote login
octonote setup claude --skill   # or: octonote setup codex
```
