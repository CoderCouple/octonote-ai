A canvas is an infinite board for boxes, arrows, sticky notes, text, drawings, images and **figures**: diagrams written as code that lay themselves out.

## Tools

The tools sit down the left edge, starting at the top. Hover one to see its name; the letter in its corner is its shortcut.

| Tool | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **+** | / or ⌘K | Insert anything: **Diagram as Code** (starter diagrams), **Diagram Catalog** (whole boards), **Shape** (incl. diamond and sticky note), **Icon**, **Device Frame** (phone, browser, …), or an empty **Figure**, a **Code Block** or an **Image**. Type to search everything. |
| **Select** | V | Pick, move and resize. Drag on empty space to select several. |
| **Rectangle / Ellipse** | R / O | Draw a box. Type to give it a label. Diamonds are under **+ › Shape**. |
| **Arrow / Line** | A / L | Drag from one box to another; the ends stay attached when the boxes move. Drag the middle handle to curve it. |
| **Pen** | D (or P) | Freehand drawing. Turn on **Auto-shape** in the header and rough strokes snap to clean shapes. |
| **Text** | T | Free text. |
| **Icon** | I | Opens the icon picker: type a name, press Enter. |
| **Figure** | F | Drag a box to make an empty diagram, then write its code. |
| **Comment** | C | Pin a comment to a shape or a spot. Type `@` to mention someone with access. |

**S** still drops a sticky note, and **H** (or holding Space) pans.

Select something to get its bar, docked at the bottom centre (it moves up out of the way if it would cover what you selected). Each button opens a small menu:

- **Colour:** border, fill (tint, solid or none) and text colours separately.
- **Stroke:** solid, dashed or dotted, four widths, and opacity.
- **Text:** size, bold, italic, font (sans, serif, mono, hand-drawn) and alignment.
- **Lines:** straight, curved or elbow, and the arrowhead at each end (none, arrow, open, dot, diamond).
- **⋯ More:** duplicate, lock, link, copy style and paste style, delete.

Right-click anything for the same actions plus cut, copy and paste, layer order, comments and export. ⌥⌘C / ⌥⌘V copy and paste a style; ⇧⌘L locks; ⌘] / ⌘[ move one layer, ⇧⌘] / ⇧⌘[ to the front or back.

**Rearrange** in the header lays out the selection (or the whole canvas) vertically or horizontally. Undo from the "Rearranged" message.

## Figures

A figure is a diagram written as code: architecture maps, flowcharts, data models (ERD), sequence diagrams and processes. Octonote lays it out, routes the arrows and keeps it tidy as it grows. The syntax is the same as [Eraser's diagram-as-code](https://docs.eraser.io/docs/syntax), so code you or an AI wrote for Eraser works here.

- **Add one** from **+** (⌘K): pick a template.
- **Turn a sketch into one:** select boxes and arrows you drew and click **To diagram**.
- **Have an agent write one:** the CLI and the Claude/ChatGPT apps draw canvases from text (see below).
- **Work inside it:** double-click (or press Enter) to drill into groups, collapse them, and play flows. Esc leaves.
- **Restyle it by clicking:** inside an opened figure, click a box, a group, an icon's label or an arrow (shift-click for several). A toolbar at the bottom changes its text, shape, icon, colour, border, text size, typeface and alignment, or an arrow's label, line, width, routing and arrowheads. Every change is written into the figure's code, and ⌘Z undoes it.
- **Edit the code:** select the figure and click **Code**. The code opens beside the canvas and the figure redraws as you type.
- **Name it** with a `title` line in its code. The title sits above the figure's border.

Embed a figure in a note with the **Figure** block, or drag it from the canvas into the note next to it in a project. The note's copy updates a few seconds after the canvas saves.

## Present

Every figure and device frame is a slide. Click **Present** (bottom right) to show them one at a time, full screen.

- Arrows, Space or a click on the strip of slides under the slide moves between them. **S** hides the strip.
- **L** turns on a laser pointer; **N** shows your speaker notes.
- The **presenter view** button opens a second window with the current slide, the next one, your notes and a timer. Keep it on your own screen.
- **Slides** (next to Present) sets the order, hides slides and holds the speaker notes.

Readers of a [published](/guides/publishing) canvas or project can present it too.

## Export

**Export** (bottom right) downloads the canvas, or just the selection, as PNG, SVG or PDF, or copies it as an image. A selected figure's bar has its own export with more formats.

## Comments

Comments live on the canvas: pins on shapes or spots, with replies, resolve and delete. Comments show the name of the person who wrote them. Resolved ones are hidden until you show them.

## Draw from text

Agents and the CLI can create a canvas from a mermaid flowchart or class diagram. Every box and arrow is a real, editable shape. See [Writing content](/agents/writing-content#canvases-mermaid-flowcharts).

<Note>The canvas follows your light or dark theme. Thumbnails and embedded figures always render on a light background.</Note>
