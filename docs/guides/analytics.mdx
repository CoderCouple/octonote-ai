---
title: "Analytics"
description: "See how many people read your published pages, without tracking them."
---

Open **Analytics** from the item's menu in any list. Owners and editors can see it.

- **Unique visitors** and **Views**, over the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 6 months or year.
- All-time visitors and views, views per visitor, when it was last viewed, and the upvotes and downvotes.

## What we count, and what we don't

- Bots and link previews aren't counted, and neither are the item's own owners and editors.
- Unique visitors are counted with a hash that changes every day. We never store IP addresses or anything else about the visitor.
- Published pages load no analytics or tracking scripts.
