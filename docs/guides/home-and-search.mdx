title: "Home and search"
description: "Find your recent work, what's shared with you, and anything by name."

The sidebar has **Home**, **Notes**, **Canvases**, **Projects**, **Notebooks**, **Shared** and **Settings**.

## Home

Home shows **Shared with you** (when someone has shared something), then your recent notes, canvases, projects and notebooks, with **View all** for each.

## Search

The search on Home finds notes, canvases, projects and notebooks **by title**, in your workspace and in what's shared with you. Press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump to it, then use <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

Search looks at titles only, not the text inside.

## Shared

**Shared** lists everything other people have shared with you, with **Your access**: **Can edit** or **Can view**.

## Deleting

Deleting a note or canvas deletes it for everyone it's shared with. Notes that link to a deleted canvas show it as unavailable. Deleting a project deletes its note and canvas too.
