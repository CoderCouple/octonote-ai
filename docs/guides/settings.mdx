Open **Settings** from the sidebar.

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your **Name**, shown as the author of pages you publish. |
| **Appearance** | Light, Dark or System. |
| **Notifications** | "Email me when someone shares with me". |
| **Privacy** | "Help improve Octonote": turn it off to stop product analytics in the app. |
| **Workspace** | Rename the workspace (owners and admins). |
| **Memberships** | Workspaces you belong to; **Switch** between them. |
| **Plan & billing** | See [Plans and billing](/guides/plans). |
| **CLI & agents** | Install the CLI, see and revoke tokens, and create a token for CI or a cloud agent. See [Cloud agents and CI](/agents/cloud-and-ci). |
| **Delete account** | Permanently delete your account. You confirm by typing your email. |

## Signing in

Continue with **Google**, **Apple**, or your **email**: we send a 6-digit code, no password needed.

## Workspaces

Create another workspace with **New workspace** in the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar. On Free you can own one workspace. Workspace roles are **Owner**, **Admin** and **Member**.

The owner can delete a workspace in **Danger zone**, confirming by typing its name.
